Give a team without kits its own kits

Football Life ships real kits for many teams, but not all. The Kits page of blue41 PES2021 Mod Studio gives any team of your game a full kit set in a minute, through the kit server your patch already uses, and lets you edit numbers, letters, colours and where the name and numbers sit.

Step by step

The app speaks English, Español and Português (Settings → Language); the names below are the ones you'll see in English.

  1. Open Kits and the Add a team tab.
  2. Search the team in your game, keep The blue41 template (recommended) or copy another team's kits, then Add team.
  3. In Team kits, pick a kit: Numbers & letters… draws the numbers and name letters in any font (or a font installed on your PC), then Replace textures.
  4. Colours & settings…: kit colours (detect from the texture or pick from the picture), collar, shirt model and Name and numbers on the kit (height, size, curve; name, chest and shorts numbers on or off), then Save settings.
  5. Start the game: the kit server loads the new kits.

Good to know

  • Every texture has Export PNG / Replace…, to edit it in any image editor.
  • Drag kits to change their order; Remove team sends a team's folder to the Recycle Bin.
  • Every change is backed up and can be undone from the Change log.
