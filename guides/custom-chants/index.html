Add custom crowd chants to PES 2021

PES 2021 plays a few fixed chant tracks per team. With the Chants page and the ChantRotator module you can give any team that has chant tracks in the game your own songs, a different one every time, without copying the 2 GB sound bank.

Step by step

The app's buttons and pages are in English; the names below are the ones you'll see.

  1. Open Chants. Included sets: + Liverpool FC and + SC Corinthians add them in one click.
  2. For another team, click + Add team and pick it (teams with chant tracks in the game).
  3. Drop MP3 or WAV files (or a whole folder) on the team, or use Add songs….
  4. Songs are converted in the background and matched to the game's own chant loudness.
  5. Set the Home chants / Away chants volume, preview, rename or switch songs on and off.
  6. Start the game: a different song plays at each match.

Good to know

  • In game, change the volume in the Sider overlay (ChantRotator_blue41): [2]/[3] home, [4]/[5] away.
  • The chant module needs Sider; the app installs it for you (Sider page → Studio modules).
  • Changes are saved straight away and heard from the next game start.
