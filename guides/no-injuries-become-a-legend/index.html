Turn off injuries in Become a Legend

Exhibition matches let you switch injuries off, but Become a Legend doesn't. The studio's No Injuries module makes the game behave as if Injuries = Off in every mode, and you can switch it from the Sider overlay at any time.

Step by step

The app's buttons and pages are in English; the names below are the ones you'll see.

  1. Open Sider → Studio modules and click Install on No Injuries (backup first, undo from the Change log).
  2. Start the game: the module is on at every game start; it applies from the next kick-off.
  3. To switch it during a session: open the Sider overlay (Space), press [1] until NoInjuries_blue41 shows, then [2].

Good to know

  • Cheats → My player keeps your own player's stamina, form and "days out" where you set them (experimental; up to about half a minute after a career loads, or [3] in the overlay).
  • The module changes nothing on disk except adding itself to Sider.
