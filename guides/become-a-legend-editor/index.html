Become a Legend editor: shirt numbers, captain, set pieces

In Become a Legend you can't change your teams' shirt numbers or keep the captain you want: the game decides. The Team page writes the numbers straight into your career save, and the Captain & set pieces module keeps your captain and set-piece takers in the game.

Step by step

The app speaks English, Español and Português (Settings → Language); the names below are the ones you'll see in English.

  1. Close the game, or stay in the main menu outside Become a Legend: the game overwrites the save when it saves.
  2. Open Become a Legend → Team and pick your save file and team.
  3. Numbers tab: change numbers with the arrows or by typing. A number another player wears shows in red with a Swap numbers button; Default numbers brings back your patch's numbers.
  4. Captain tab: choose the captain. The Captain & set pieces Sider module keeps him in the game, even after the "club made you captain" event.
  5. Press Save (backup first), then load your career.

Good to know

  • Set pieces tab: pick the takers (long and short free kick, second free-kick taker, left / right corner, penalty); the same module keeps them in the game. If you pick them yourself in the match Game Plan (Full team control), switch the module's lock off ([5] in the overlay).
  • After a career loads, the module needs up to about half a minute to find your team; Sider's overlay applies it at once (BALCaptain_blue41, key [6]).
  • Every save change is listed in the Change log, where one click puts the save back.
