Modding Football Life 2026 (FL26) with blue41 Mod Studio

SP Football Life 2026 is a standalone football game built on PES 2021 by the SmokePatch community, and it comes with Sider. That makes it a perfect fit for the studio: the app was built and tested on Football Life 2026, and everything on this site works there.

Step by step

The app's buttons and pages are in English; the names below are the ones you'll see.

  1. Download the app and unzip it anywhere (no installer).
  2. Run blue41.ModStudio.exe. Home finds your Football Life folder, its Sider and your saves; if not, click Choose… and pick the game folder.
  3. Install the studio modules you want (Sider → Studio modules): chants, No Injuries, captain & set pieces, national numbers, My player.
  4. Use the guides: boots and gloves packs, chants, Become a Legend editor.
  5. Start the game: changes load at launch.

Good to know

  • Your boots, chants and modules live in the studio's own folders, so a Football Life update doesn't wipe them; when the patch's files change, the Boots & gloves page offers to rebuild on the new ones.
  • Football Life 2027 (FL27): we'll test it and update the studio as soon as it's out.
  • The studio isn't made by SmokePatch; it works with your own installation and ships no game files.
