Sider 7 settings explained (sider.ini, modules, livecpk)

Sider (by juce) loads mods into PES 2021 without touching the game's files: content roots (livecpk folders) and Lua modules, all set in a text file, sider.ini. One wrong line or the wrong order and a mod silently stops working. The Sider page shows every option in plain words.

Step by step

The app's buttons and pages are in English; the names below are the ones you'll see.

  1. Open Sider; the app finds sider.ini on its own (or choose the Sider folder on Home).
  2. Settings: each option with an explanation and the right control: switches, key pickers, the installed-fonts list, a colour picker.
  3. Modules and Content roots: switch Lua modules and livecpk folders on or off and drag them into order. When two roots have the same file, the one higher in the list wins.
  4. Text: the whole sider.ini with a before / after comparison of your changes.
  5. Save (backup first), then restart the game: Sider reads its settings at launch.

Good to know

  • The Become a Legend modules need Sider's LuaJIT option on (luajit.ext.enabled = 1).
  • Don't have Sider? Home has a Get Sider… link. Many patches, like SP Football Life, already include it.
  • Studio modules: the studio's own modules install in one click, and Home tells you when they need an update.
