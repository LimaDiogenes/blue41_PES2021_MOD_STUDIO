Install a boots or gloves pack in PES 2021

Boot and glove packs usually come as a zip with dozens of model folders, menu pictures and a player list. Copying them by hand overwrites files and mixes up the game's boots menu. The Boots & gloves page of blue41 PES2021 Mod Studio does the install for you, shows what each new item replaces, and can undo it.

Step by step

The app's buttons and pages are in English; the names below are the ones you'll see.

  1. Open Boots & gloves and go to the Import pack tab.
  2. Choose the pack: a zip, 7z or rar file, or an unpacked folder.
  3. Review the list: what's new, what would replace one of yours, and what you already have. New items replace yours by line (the same model, a newer colourway), and the menu place and the players move with them.
  4. Optional: pick another item to replace, or Choose a place! when nothing matches. Tick Delete replaced items to send the old ones to the Recycle Bin, and choose whether to use the new pack's player list.
  5. Press Install. A progress bar shows each step; the app asks before writing anything.
  6. Start the game: Sider loads the new boots and gloves at launch.

Good to know

  • Your boots live in the studio's own Sider folder (livecpk\ModStudio-Gear), so a patch update doesn't wipe them.
  • Players you edited in the game's Edit mode are updated too: the last edit wins, in the app or in the game.
  • Made a mistake? Undo (top right of the page) or the Change log puts every file back.
  • No menu picture in the pack? Make one with the built-in generator: your logos, 13 free fonts and colours taken from the texture.
