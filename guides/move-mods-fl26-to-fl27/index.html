Move your mods from FL26 to FL27

Copying mods between two Football Life versions by hand means comparing folders and editing sider.ini. The Move from another game page compares both games, shows only what the new one doesn't have, and copies what you tick.

Step by step

The app speaks English, Español and Português (Settings → Language); the names below are the ones you'll see in English.

  1. On Home, pick the game you are moving to (FL27) and close it if it's running.
  2. Open Move from another game and check Copy from: (FL26). The comparison can take a few minutes on big installs.
  3. Needs your decision: for each FL26 module that does a job FL27 already does, choose Don't move it (safest), Move its content (its kits, balls or stadiums go into FL27's own module) or Copy and use both (not recommended: they can clash).
  4. Tick what else you want from Content roots (livecpk), Lua modules and Game data files. Nothing is pre-ticked: these can be tens of GB.
  5. Press Copy ticked… and confirm (backup of sider.ini first), then start the game.

Good to know

  • Nothing of FL27 is overwritten and FL26 stays as it is. Where both games have an entry for the same team or competition, FL27's stays. Undo from the Change log.
  • Face packs: FL27 ships empty placeholder files, and the page replaces those with your FL26 pack.
  • Your career: Become a Legend → Move a career (experimental) writes your FL26 career as a new FL27 save. Keep your own copy of the original.
  • Not moved yet: scoreboards, referee kits, sleeve badges, menus and your boots, chants and ball choices made in the studio. The page's Not moved by this page card explains how to do each by hand.
  • Gameplay hooks and exe patches are made for one game exe: get the version made for the new game from its author.
